What we do

Three ways a coil leaves our plant.

Every order starts as a mother coil from JSW Steel, SAIL, Tata Steel or your own supply. It leaves as sheet, plate or slit coil, ready for your press, laser or tube mill.

ProcessesCapabilitiesMaterialsHow to order

Cut-to-length

Sheets and plates, laser-flat.

The strip runs through 4-Hi and 6-Hi levellers that flex it until edge and centre waviness are gone, then a flying shear cuts it to length as it feeds. The result stays flat after laser cutting.

  • Thickness0.3–16 mm
  • Sheet length0.8–18 m
  • Accuracy±1 mm/m
  • Max coil40 MT

Slitting

Slit coils, clean edges.

Rotary knives cut the mother coil lengthwise into narrow strips. A separator keeps them apart and the recoiler winds each strip into its own coil on a 610 mm ID. Coated and stainless material is slit without marking.

  • Thickness0.6–8 mm
  • Strips per passup to 12
  • Width tolerance+0.5 mm
  • Telescope≤ 20 mm

Cut-to-size

Blanks to your drawing.

Narrow strip and slit coil cut into precise flats and blanks. Small 350 mm pieces and 12 m plates run on the same machine, so mixed orders ship together.

  • Blank lengthfrom 350 mm
  • Plate lengthto 12 m
  • Narrow HR width100–800 mm
  • Accuracy±1 mm/m

How to order

From enquiry to dispatch in four steps.

You only do the first step. We handle the rest and keep you posted.

  1. Send your spec

    Size, grade, quantity and packing, by the form or email.

  2. We pick the line

    We match your coil to the right line and confirm a delivery date.

  3. Process and inspect

    Levelled, cut and camera-checked on both surfaces.

  4. Weigh and dispatch

    Weighed on our own weighbridge, packed and sent across India.
